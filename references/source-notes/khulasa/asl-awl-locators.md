## Printed page 279 — أصل المسألة

The page lists the seven standard fixed-share case origins as 2, 3, 4, 6, 8,
12, and 24. Its table derives them from the denominators of the fixed shares
that coexist in a case.

## Printed pages 281–283 — العول

The source states that only origins 6, 12, and 24 undergo awl, and gives these
worked examples:

| Origin → awl | Source-worked heirs | Original saham |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6 → 7 | husband; two sisters | 3; 4 collectively |
| 6 → 8 | husband; two sisters; mother | 3; 4 collectively; 1 |
| 6 → 9 | husband; two sisters; mother; one uterine sibling | 3; 4 collectively; 1; 1 |
| 6 → 10 | husband; two sisters; mother; two uterine siblings | 3; 4 collectively; 1; 2 collectively |
| 12 → 13 | wife; mother; two sisters | 3; 2; 8 collectively |
| 12 → 15 | wife; mother; two sisters; one uterine sibling | 3; 2; 8 collectively; 2 |
| 12 → 17 | wife; mother; two sisters; two uterine siblings | 3; 2; 8 collectively; 4 collectively |
| 24 → 27 | wife; father; mother; two daughters | 3; 4; 4; 16 collectively |

The tables preserve the original saham and replace the case denominator with
their exact sum. This note is a short structured paraphrase, not a quotation.
